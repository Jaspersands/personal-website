Auto-tuning a double quantum dot

a toy of what my thesis automates

In silicon quantum computers, qubits are encoded in the spins of isolated single electrons. Before you can compute, you have to initialize the system into the (1,1) charge state (one electron in dot 1, one electron in dot 2). Doing this by hand is a bottleneck that takes hours.

This simulator models a double quantum dot read out via a nearby charge sensor. The tuner is blind to the 2D honeycomb—it only measures 1D sensor blips. It empties both dots down to (0,0), adds one electron to each dot using change-point detection, and locks safely in the center of the (1,1) cell.
